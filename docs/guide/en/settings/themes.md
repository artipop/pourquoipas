# Light and dark

The app has one look, made from its icon: in the light half, cream paper and
navy text; in the dark half, a navy ground and cream text. Mustard marks the
open board and the cards where an agent is waiting for an answer.

Only the side is chosen: "Settings → The app itself", the **"Light or dark"**
field:

- **"Light"**;
- **"Dark"**;
- **"As the system is"** — the board turns light and dark with the system.

The choice applies at once, is kept by the install, survives a restart and
holds on a phone through [Access from a phone](./index.md#access-from-a-phone).
